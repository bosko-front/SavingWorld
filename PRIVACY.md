# Saving Worlds Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Saving Worlds is an iPhone app for tracking money you set aside for your own savings goals. Each goal grows a small world. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. It is published by Bosko Jaksic, the developer of Saving Worlds ("we").

## In short

- Your goals, savings entries, notes and worlds are stored only on your iPhone.
- Saving Worlds has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. We never receive your goals, amounts or notes.
- The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not sell or share your data.
- The app connects to the internet in only two cases: when Apple handles an App Store purchase, and when the app checks for an app update.

## Information stored on your iPhone

Saving Worlds keeps the following in a private database inside the app on your iPhone:

- your goals: name, target amount, optional deadline, chosen world and status;
- the savings entries you record: amount, deposit or withdrawal, date and an optional note;
- your souvenirs (finished worlds) and quest history;
- your settings: currency and the weekly reminder switch;
- a record of whether Saving Worlds Pro is active on this iPhone, and the App Store transaction identifiers needed to check it.

This information never leaves your iPhone through Saving Worlds. We cannot see it, access it or restore it.

The app does not connect to a bank and does not hold, move or check any money. Everything you record is entered by you.

## Purchases

Saving Worlds Pro is a one-time In-App Purchase sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and your Apple Account details; we never receive your name, email address or payment information.

To confirm a purchase, the app uses Apple's StoreKit. StoreKit gives the app the product and transaction details, already verified by Apple. The app stores them on your iPhone only. Restore Purchases asks the App Store for your existing purchases and turns Pro back on. It cannot restore your goals or savings entries.

Apple's handling of purchases is described in the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## App updates

Saving Worlds uses Expo's EAS Update service to deliver small app updates. When the app starts, it may ask Expo's servers whether an update is available. Each such request sends technical information only:

- the platform (iOS);
- the app's name and build number, and the version of iOS's network software, as sent by iOS with every app request;
- your iPhone's preferred language, as sent by iOS with every app request;
- the app's runtime version and update channel;
- the identifiers of the app version and update currently installed;
- a random identifier created when the app was installed. It is not linked to your name, your Apple Account or your savings data. It is used to deliver and count updates.
- if the app stopped because of an error during its previous launch, a short technical error message.

Like any internet request, this also reveals your device's IP address to Expo. These requests contain none of your goals, amounts or notes. Expo's handling of this information is described in the [Expo Privacy Policy](https://expo.dev/privacy).

## Notifications

The optional weekly reminder is a local notification scheduled on your iPhone. The app asks for notification permission only when you turn the reminder on in Settings. The reminder's text is general and contains no amounts or goal names. No push notification service is used, and no notification data is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn the reminder off in Saving Worlds Settings, or turn notifications off in iOS Settings.

## Device information used on the device

The app reads your iPhone's language and region settings to show numbers, dates and your local currency in a familiar format. It also follows accessibility settings such as text size and Reduce Motion. This happens on your iPhone. Apart from the preferred language that iOS adds to update checks (see App updates), none of it is sent anywhere.

## Backups and deleting your data

Saving Worlds has no backup, export or sync feature, and it does not upload your data. Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone. If you delete the app or lose your iPhone, your savings records may be permanently lost, and we cannot recover them.

iOS may include app data in iPhone or iCloud backups that you make. Apple manages those backups under its own terms, not Saving Worlds.

To delete individual goals or entries, use the delete options in the app. To delete everything, delete the app from your iPhone.

## Children

Saving Worlds is a general-audience app. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes, we will update this policy and the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
