Saving Worlds is a personal savings tracker. There is no account or sign-in, no server of our own and no bank connection: people type the amounts they set aside themselves. All records are stored on the device and every feature except the App Store purchase works offline. No demo account is needed.

HOW TO REVIEW
1. Onboarding: Next, Next, choose a currency, Get Started.
2. Create a goal (name, target amount, optional deadline). Home shows the goal's Island. Tap "Add savings" to record a deposit; a new part of the world unlocks at every 10% of the target.
3. Home's "..." button (Goal options): History (correct or delete entries), Edit goal, Change world, Savings insights (Pro), New goal, Pause goal, Finish goal (shown once the target is reached), Delete goal.
4. Challenges tab: three fixed quests (First step, Next milestone, Weekly step; Weekly step needs a deadline).
5. Collection tab: a souvenir appears when a goal first reaches 100% of its target.
6. Settings (gear icon on Home): currency, weekly reminder (a local notification on Sundays at 18:00; permission is asked only when it is turned on), Your data, Saving Worlds Pro.

IN-APP PURCHASE
Saving Worlds Pro (com.bosko123.savingworlds.pro.lifetime) is a one-time non-consumable purchase. No subscription.
- To open the Pro screen: Settings > Saving Worlds Pro, or Home > "..." > New goal while one goal exists ("Build another world" > Explore Pro), or the locked Garden card when choosing a world.
- Restore Purchases is on the same Pro screen.
- Pro unlocks: unlimited goals, the Garden world (choose it when creating a goal, or Home > "..." > Change world), switching a goal between Island and Garden, and Savings insights (Home > "..." > Savings insights).
- The quests and the weekly reminder are identical for free and Pro users.

NETWORK
The app connects only to the App Store (StoreKit) and to Expo's update service (EAS Update) to check for app updates. No goals, amounts or notes are sent anywhere.
