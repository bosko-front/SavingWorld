# Saving Worlds — start here

Saving Worlds is an offline, iOS-first Expo app for manually tracking real savings toward goals that grow small worlds. Every session starts from the repository documentation, not from earlier chats.

## Authority

1. The owner's current instruction in this session.
2. **`docs/Saving_Worlds_MVP_Plan_v1_2.docx` (23 October 2026) is the supreme product authority.** Read its full text and tables with a local reader, for example `textutil -convert txt -stdout docs/Saving_Worlds_MVP_Plan_v1_2.docx`. If it cannot be read, report that blocker; never fall back to v1.1.
3. Current normalized docs: `docs/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md`, `docs/SOURCE_RECONCILIATION.md`, `docs/DECISIONS.md` and the engineering documents they link.
4. Historical sources (`docs/sources/`, v1.1, 22 October feature decisions, old evidence) never override v1.2, even where they say "confirmed".

Q01–Q06 are **resolved** by v1.2. Do not ask for reapproval and do not restore custom challenges, per-goal currencies or entry audit/version history. No automatic data loss, ever (D75): a lost Pro entitlement or any automatic process never deletes or changes goals, entries, souvenirs or theme choices; deleting is only the person's own confirmed action, and a Garden goal without Pro stays as it is (locked) until the person chooses Island themselves. Owner decision D74 (23 October 2026) overrides v1.2 on goals: Archive and Close goal are removed (statuses active, paused, completed), any goal can be deleted after a native confirmation (goal, entries and souvenir; no undo), Free may have one goal in total in any status, and loss of Pro keeps one chosen goal usable and the rest read-only. Do not restore Archive or the old no-deletion rule. Owner decision D118 (26 October 2026) overrides v1.2 on one Garden unlock: 80% is the greenhouse and vegetable beds, not a gazebo (the 60% fountain brings all the water; the pergola is part of 90%). Only Q07 (price/sale copy) and Q09 (identity/operational access) remain release dependencies; Q08 is future-only.
