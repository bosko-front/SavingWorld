## Frequently asked questions

### Does Saving Worlds move or hold my money?

No. Saving Worlds is a record of money you set aside yourself. It does not connect to a bank and cannot make or check payments.

### Where are my goals and savings stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no cloud copy. See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) for details.

### Can I back up my data or move it to a new iPhone?

Saving Worlds has no backup, export or sync feature. Deleting the app or losing your iPhone can permanently remove your savings records, and we cannot recover them. iPhone and iCloud backups are made by iOS, not by Saving Worlds, so the app cannot promise that your records return from them.

### I bought Saving Worlds Pro. How do I get it back after reinstalling or on a new iPhone?

Open Saving Worlds, go to Settings › Saving Worlds Pro and tap **Restore Purchases**. Sign in with the same Apple Account you used for the purchase. Restore brings back Pro. It does not bring back goals or savings entries.

### How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). If Pro is refunded, your goals and entries stay on your iPhone. If you have more than one goal, you choose one to keep using; the others stay visible but read-only. Nothing is deleted.

### Can I change my currency?

You can change the currency in Settings until you save your first entry and while no goal has a souvenir. After that it stays locked. Amounts are never converted.

### The weekly reminder doesn't arrive.

Turn on Weekly reminder in Saving Worlds Settings, and allow notifications for Saving Worlds in iOS Settings › Notifications. The reminder arrives on Sundays at 6:00 PM (18:00) on your iPhone's time while at least one goal is active. Paused or finished goals don't trigger it.

### Can I delete a goal?

Yes. Open the goal's options and choose Delete. Deleting removes the goal, all its savings entries and its finished world, and it cannot be undone.
