# Product decisions supplied after version 1.1

Owner clarification, 22 October 2026. These decisions override ambiguous or conflicting statements in the version 1.1 product plan. This file records product content; it does not authorize application implementation during the planning task.

## Challenges

Free MVP includes basic savings challenges and weekly savings targets. Pro also allows custom challenges with configurable amounts and frequency. XP, levels, complex missions and special rewards are postponed.

## Statistics

Free includes current saved amount, completion percentage, remaining amount, deposit and withdrawal history, and required weekly/monthly savings when a deadline exists.

Pro also includes total savings across all goals, monthly deposit and withdrawal summaries, a simple savings progress chart, and a completed goals overview. Advanced forecasting and financial analytics are out of scope.

## Personalization

Free users configure goal name, target, currency and optional deadline. Island progresses automatically. Pro users choose Island or Garden per goal and can change the theme later. Manual placement, decoration editing, color customization and advanced world-building controls are out of scope.

## Visual progression

| Progress | Island | Garden |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0% | Empty island | Empty garden |
| 10% | First tree | First seedlings |
| 20% | Path | Small flower bed |
| 30% | Flowers and bushes | More flowers |
| 40% | Small house | Decorative tree |
| 50% | Bridge | Garden pathway |
| 60% | Pond | Small fountain |
| 70% | Additional trees | Bench and decorations |
| 80% | Larger house and details | Gazebo |
| 90% | Final decorations | Final plants and ornaments |
| 100% | Completed island | Completed garden |

Actual deposits and withdrawals determine progress. Decreased savings reduce the visible scene while preserving transaction history. Progression is shared across themes and must support future premium themes without a major redesign.

The final Pro price and future premium-theme entitlement rules remain unresolved. Do not start implementation during this planning task.
