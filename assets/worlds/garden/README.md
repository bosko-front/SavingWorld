# Garden world — Blender source workspace

Source art for the Pro **Garden** theme (docs/ASSET_SPEC.md, PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS R11). This folder holds the editable Blender scene and the scripts that generate it. It is **not** read by the app: approved exports go to `assets/themes/garden/` in Phase 17, with a manifest, exactly like the Island (D76, D77).

Status (26 October 2026): **Step 2 of the owner's Garden brief — the completed (100%) Garden is built and rendered for visual approval.** Not approved yet. No milestone renders, layer export, manifest or app integration exist yet (Steps 3-5 wait for approval). The milestone order is still an open owner decision (see "Milestone sequence").

## Reuse of the Island pipeline

The Garden is generated by its own package, `scripts/swg/`, which imports the Island's shared modules from `assets/worlds/island/scripts/swi/` read-only (nothing in the Island folder changes):

| Island module | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| `swi/rig.py` | The **same frozen rig**: orthographic camera 57°/0°/45°, ortho scale 35.2, 2048×1536 (4:3), sun, fill, bounce, sky, Cycles/AgX settings. Both worlds share projection, light and shadows (ASSET_SPEC "Match camera angle, lighting… across both themes"). |
| `swi/stylize.py` | The approved storybook pass (light bands, soft Freestyle outlines, Kuwahara), via `rig.configure_render` |
| `swi/materials.py` | Painted, terrain, rock, wood, foliage, water (Flow UV), waterfall, cloud and emissive shaders |
| `swi/common.py` | `Geo` primitives, metaball merging, seeded noise, colors |
| `swi/nature.py`, `swi/terrain.py`, `swi/props.py` | Generic builders: broadleaf/pine trees, bushes, flowers, cloud shapes, stone stacks, grass tufts, foam, floating islets |
| `swi/preview.py` | Backdrop composite, phone-size copies, contact sheets |

Garden-specific code lives in `scripts/swg/`: `layout.py` (every position, single source of truth, in screen-plan coordinates `(v, u)`), `terrain.py`, `water.py`, `plants.py`, `structures.py`, `stages.py`, `style.py` (no-outline marks).

## Layout

| Path | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `reference/garden-reference.webp` | The owner's visual reference (direction only; nothing traced, sampled or embedded) |
| `scripts/build_garden.py` | Rebuilds the whole scene from scratch and saves `source/garden.blend` (`SW_ONLY_STAGES=0,6` builds a subset) |
| `scripts/render_preview.py` | High-quality render of one stage (2048×1536, 256 samples) plus backdrop and phone-size copies; `-- 10 256 <dir> 1` also shows the ambient-only butterflies |
| `scripts/draft_render.py` | Fast low-sample render for iteration |
| `scripts/check_framing.py` | Reports whether a stage stays inside the shared frozen camera frame |
| `source/garden.blend` | Editable scene (generated, compressed) |
| `renders/review/` | Renders submitted for owner review (committed) |
| `renders/preview/` | Ad-hoc renders (not committed) |

## Rebuild and render

Blender 5.2.3 LTS (`/Applications/Blender.app`), Cycles on the Apple M4 GPU (Metal).

```bash
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --factory-startup -P assets/worlds/garden/scripts/build_garden.py
```

```bash
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b assets/worlds/garden/source/garden.blend -P assets/worlds/garden/scripts/render_preview.py -- 10 256 renders/review 1
```

The build is deterministic (seeded random numbers, fixed noise offsets). Edit the scripts, not the `.blend`.

## Composition

A rounder floating garden than the Island's squarish rock, on three lawn terraces held by **dry-stone retaining walls** (the Garden's signature: a cultivated, terraced garden rather than a wild island). Screen-plan positions (`v` right, `u` into the picture):

- **Back terrace:** the glass **greenhouse** (hero, door facing the path, plants and warm lights inside, planters in front), raised **vegetable beds** with a wooden fence on the left, a white **picket fence** on the right, the stone **fountain**, and the pink **blossom tree**.
- **Middle terrace:** the **seedling plot** (left), the **flower bed** (center), the **rill** coming down from the fountain, and the **rose pergola** with a bench and a hanging lantern (right).
- **Front terrace:** the **lily pond** with koi, fed by a cascade over the front wall; its outflow **stream** runs under the wooden **bridge** and leaves over the front-left lip as the **waterfall**; a bench by the pond, a birdbath, the seating **nook** (left).
- **Main path:** paved with cream flagstones from the front edge up two flights of stone stairs (through gaps in the walls) to the greenhouse door, with four lanterns.
- **Edges and underside:** rim shrubs and flowering bushes, trees around the back and sides, ivy on the walls, flowering vines hanging over the warm rock underside, three floating islets, three clouds (one catches the waterfall).

## Milestone sequence (open owner decision)

The product authority (MVP plan v1.2 section 5, PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS, ASSET_SPEC) fixes the Garden labels; the owner's Garden brief proposed a different order. The recommended order keeps v1.2 everywhere except one label (80%):

| % | MVP plan v1.2 (current authority) | Owner's brief | Recommended (built) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Empty garden | Base floating terrain, grass and basic landscape | Empty garden (terraces, walls, lawn) |
| 10 | First seedlings | First seedlings and small plants | First seedlings |
| 20 | Small flower bed | Stone paths and first flower bed | Small flower bed |
| 30 | More flowers | Additional flowers and bushes | More flowers |
| 40 | Decorative tree | Decorative tree and garden details | Decorative tree (pink blossom) |
| 50 | Garden pathway | Main garden pathway and small bridge | Garden pathway (stairs included) |
| 60 | Small fountain | Pond, stream and waterfall | Small fountain **and the water it feeds** (rill, pond, stream, bridge, waterfall) |
| 70 | Bench and decorations | Bench, lanterns and decorative details | Bench and decorations |
| 80 | **Gazebo** | Greenhouse and vegetable beds | **Greenhouse and vegetable beds** (needs a product change) |
| 90 | Final plants and ornaments | Flower-covered pergola and additional decorations | Final plants and ornaments (rose pergola, trees, shrubs, ivy) |
| 100 | Completed garden | Completed garden with final flowers and finishing details | Completed garden |

Why: the fountain is the source of all the water, so "small fountain" at 60% can bring the pond, stream and waterfall with it (the owner's 60%) without renaming; the bridge waits for its stream (so 50% never shows a bridge over dry lawn); the greenhouse is the garden's hero building and lands where the Island's larger house lands (80%); the pergola is a planted ornament and fits "final plants and ornaments". Until the owner decides, the product documents keep v1.2 and nothing here is final. A changed sequence becomes a new DECISIONS entry with ASSET_SPEC and PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS updated together.

## Animation readiness (for Steps 4-5)

Separate objects were planned from the start so the export can cut ambient layers without masks where possible:

| Ambient element | Objects | Planned motion (existing Reanimated ambient system, D90-D93) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trees | `Tree_<name>_Trunk` + `Tree_<name>_Canopy` per tree (13 trees incl. the blossom tree); trunk base = sway pivot | Canopy-only sway (trunk stays), like the Island's per-tree `sway` |
| Water | `Water_Rill`, `Water_Stream`, `Water_Pond`, `Water_Fountain`, `Waterfall`, `Water_FountainCurtain` with the Island's Flow UV | Three flow layers per the Island's D93 method (streaks travel along the flow; no blinking opacity on the whole surface) |
| Koi | `Koi_1`, `Koi_2` | Slow looping glide in the pond |
| Butterflies | `Butterfly_1..3` in `SWG_Ambient` | Occasional short flight, one at a time |
| Lanterns | `Lantern_Glow_1..4`, `Lantern_Glow_Pergola`, `Greenhouse_LightGlow` | Very subtle warm glow change |
| Clouds | `Cloud_1..3` | Drift, as on the Island |
| Falling petals | from the blossom tree (to be added as a few export-only petal sprites) | Occasional single petal |

Every ambient value must rest at 0 so the still stack equals the approved picture (as for the Island), run only on a visible Home with the app active, stop under Reduce Motion, and be measured on a physical iPhone in Phase 21.

## Export and performance plan (Step 4)

- Same export as the Island (`export_layers.py` approach): base plus one cropped transparent part per separate object per milestone, positions and anchors on the 2048×1536 canvas, `2048/` master and `1536/` app size, manifest version with ambient marks validated by `src/features/worlds/manifest.ts`. The Garden keeps the 4:3 frame (`WORLD_SCENE_ASPECT`) and the shared camera, so the app's framing code needs no change.
- Budget: ASSET_SPEC's ~32 MiB decoded per world. The Island is 13.7 MiB at 1536 with 49 layers. The Garden will have more small parts (13 trees, water flow layers, koi, butterflies, glows); crops stay small, so the estimate is 15-20 MiB at 1536. This is an estimate to measure at export, not a result; the physical-iPhone memory and frame check stays in Phase 21 (as D88/D90 for the Island).
- Render cost: one full-quality Garden state takes about 8.5 minutes on the M4 (501 s at 256 samples, glass and dense foliage), against about 4.5 minutes for an Island state.

## Review log

### 26 October 2026 — Step 2: completed Garden for approval

Files in `renders/review/`: `garden_stage10_preview.png` (transparent 2048×1536 master), `_backdrop.png`, the iPhone 17 Pro and SE sizes, and `garden_reference_comparison.png` (reference left, render right). Rendered with the ambient-only butterflies visible so they can be judged; milestone renders leave them out.

Checks: the scene builds from scratch deterministically (`build_garden.py`, about 25-120 s); everything stays inside the shared frame (`check_framing.py`: x 0.065-0.868, y 0.099-0.930; Island 0.108-0.896 / 0.080-0.965); the SE-size copy keeps the greenhouse, blossom tree, fountain, rill, pond, waterfall, path and walls readable.

Main differences from the reference:

1. **Density.** The reference is more overgrown (flower masses everywhere, almost no open lawn). The render keeps more lawn so it reads at phone size and so every milestone visibly adds something; it can be made lusher if the owner wants.
2. **Water.** The reference has a natural rocky cascade into a large central pond. The render brings the water from a stone fountain down a stone-lined rill with two cascades into a smaller lily pond, so the water can arrive with the "small fountain" milestone without changing the base terrain.
3. **Terraces.** The reference stacks many small, irregular levels. The render has three broad terraces with long dry-stone walls, which keeps the stage-0 terrain simple and identical in every state; the walls are more prominent than in the reference.
4. **Style.** The reference is a painted illustration. The render uses the Island's approved procedural storybook look, so the two worlds match; fine painted texture detail (stone and leaf texture, warm interior light) is softer.
5. **Smaller details.** The greenhouse's interior light is faint (reference: clearly lit); the pergola reads as a rose-covered frame but its bench is hidden; the seating nook is small; the underside is similar to the Island's (warmer stone, more vines).
